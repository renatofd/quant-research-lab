# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

Contributors and maintainers are committed to an open, welcoming,
harassment-free community regardless of experience, background, identity,
disability, appearance, religion, or technical viewpoint.

## Expected Behavior

- Discuss Evidence, methodology, and code without attacking individuals.
- State uncertainty and correct mistakes transparently.
- Respect preregistered decisions, including negative results.
- Provide specific, actionable feedback.
- Protect personal data, credentials, and confidential reports.
- Disclose relevant conflicts of interest.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, threats, discrimination, or disclosure of personal information.
- Manipulation of results or pressure to suppress Evidence.
- Publication of credentials or confidential material.
- Personal attacks, trolling, or deliberate disruption.
- Misleading financial claims based on this repository.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, remove, or reject comments, commits, issues, and
contributions that violate this Code of Conduct. Participation may be limited
when necessary, using a proportionate and documented response.

Routine moderation concerns may be reported through an issue. If public
disclosure could cause harm, use GitHub's private security reporting channel
and identify the report as a conduct matter rather than a technical
vulnerability.

Reports will be handled as confidentially, impartially, and respectfully as
possible. Retaliation against a good-faith reporter is not tolerated.
