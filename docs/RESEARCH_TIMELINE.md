## How to Read the Timeline

Dates identify decisions and Research Artifacts, not a story of steadily
improving performance. Several lines ended early because a minimal experiment
had already reduced their rational probability of success below the scientific
budget threshold.

Stopping early was part of the Research Protocol, not an implementation
failure.
