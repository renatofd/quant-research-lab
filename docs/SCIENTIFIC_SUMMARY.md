# Scientific Summary

## Research Question

Does the available Evidence support a real, statistically defensible,
replicable, and operationally viable economic edge?

## Answer

**No—not under the tested data, periods, assets, timeframes, costs, and Research
Protocols.**

## Evidence

- Technical strategy families failed Walk-Forward Analysis, robustness, or
  survival gates.
- Raw OHLCV effects remained negative after costs.
- Volatility persistence and cross-sectional dispersion predicted aspects of
  risk, but did not produce an approved directional edge.
- BTC lead–lag effects were not sufficiently specific or economically useful.
- Seasonality, volume, and the OHLCV illiquidity proxy failed their incremental
  Evidence gates.
- Spot–perpetual divergence and cross-exchange leadership were statistically
  measurable but too small to be economically useful.
- Liquidation cascades did not replicate with sufficient magnitude.
- The remaining Hypotheses require data the laboratory does not possess.

## What the Evidence Does Not Establish

The project does not claim:

- that crypto markets contain no inefficiencies;
- that materially new data could never reveal a mechanism;
- that risk forecasting has no value;
- that the negative results extend beyond their registered scope.

## What the Evidence Does Establish

- No existing component justifies deployment with real capital.
- Searching the same data for new parameters would increase overfitting risk.
- Microstructure mechanisms should not be inferred from OHLCV proxies.
- A future research generation requires materially new data and a prospective
  Holdout.
- Stopping operational development was the correct scientific decision.
