# Roadmap

## Current Status

Version 1.0.0 records a completed research program. The Roadmap does not include
bot development, parameter optimization, or new variants of rejected
Hypotheses.

## Near Term: Maintenance

- Maintain documentation, tests, and Reproducibility.
- Enable and monitor private vulnerability reporting.
- Support independent audits of links, hashes, and decisions.
- Add integrity checks for Scientific Ledgers and schemas.
- Document a sustainable process for external dataset deposits.

## Medium Term: Reproducibility

- Package data manifests in a platform-independent format.
- Add automated mechanical checks that never compute outcomes.
- Record errata without overwriting historical Research Artifacts.
- Track security-relevant Freqtrade updates without reopening scientific work.

## Conditions for Future Research

A new research generation may begin only when a point-in-time, auditable dataset
is economically necessary for a genuinely new Hypothesis. Priority data classes
are:

1. sequenced order books paired with trades;
2. on-chain labels versioned by knowledge date;
3. official or independently audited ETF flow vintages;
4. executable historical options surfaces;
5. a prospectively reserved Holdout defined before research design.

## Explicitly Out of Scope

- popular indicators or arbitrary indicator combinations;
- Hyperopt for closed Hypotheses;
- revised cost assumptions intended to rescue a result;
- new horizons or thresholds selected after outcomes;
- Ensembles assembled from economically negative effects;
- operational deployment without scientific promotion.

## Gate for Discovery V4

Discovery V4 can exist only if:

- new data resolves a specific causal blockage;
- provenance, licensing, and cost are acceptable;
- a minimal experiment can falsify the Hypothesis;
- expected information value exceeds scientific cost and complexity;
- the Research Protocol and decision criteria are frozen before outcomes;
- an independent Holdout can be preserved.

Until these conditions are met, maintenance and independent verification remain
the only active workstreams.
