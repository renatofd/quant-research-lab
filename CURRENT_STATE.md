# Historical State: Beginning of Phase 3

> **Archived snapshot:** this document records the transition into Phase 3. It
> is not the current scientific status. The research program has since
> concluded; see the [Scientific Summary](docs/SCIENTIFIC_SUMMARY.md).

## State at the Time

- Research infrastructure: complete
- Research Pipeline: complete
- observational phase: complete
- primary limitation: observational reports could no longer answer causal
  questions

The remaining questions concerned controlled interventions:

- Did ATR improve trade quality?
- Did volume improve Generalization?
- Did breakout logic provide independent information?

Historical reports could not determine the outcomes of opportunities blocked by
those components.

## Phase 3 Direction

Phase 3 therefore introduced controlled intervention. Each experiment changed
one component while holding every other variable fixed. New strategy
architectures were prohibited unless causal Evidence justified them.

The registered priorities were:

1. adaptive ATR;
2. volume relaxation;
3. breakout relaxation;
4. adaptive thresholds;
5. regime-aware filters.

Success was evaluated through:

- Walk-Forward Analysis;
- survival rate;
- stability;
- robustness;
- Generalization.

Subsequent phases closed these operational families and moved to economic
Hypotheses. The final project conclusion remains that no robust edge justified
an operational bot.
